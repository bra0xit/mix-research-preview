02THE QUESTION

Identify fish species — from a single water sample

With eDNA metabarcoding, we can identify fish species in a lake, river or coastal site from a simple water sample. The method captures DNA traces that fish release into the water through mucus, skin cells and faeces.

  • Sampling takes approximately 30 minutes per site
  • An analysis typically identifies 10–30+ species per sample
  • Results are compared with reference databases (NCBI and our own databases)
  • Quality assurance using positive and negative controls
  • Environmental monitoring and follow-up of rivers
  • Evidence for permitting and environmental impact assessments
  • Fish surveys at migration barriers
  • Ecological status assessment (EU Water Framework Directive)
  • Monitoring restoration outcomes
  • Offshore surveys for wind energy projects
