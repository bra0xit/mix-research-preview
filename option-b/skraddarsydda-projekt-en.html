Bespoke eDNA projects

Need a unique solution? We design tailored studies — from offshore wind projects to population genetics and long-term monitoring programmes.

01SERVICE
02THE QUESTION

Unique questions need unique solutions

Not every project fits a standard service. We design bespoke eDNA studies for your specific questions — from offshore wind projects in the Baltic Sea and population genetics of threatened species to long-term monitoring programmes with hundreds of sampling sites.

  • Offshore eDNA surveys of fish and marine mammals for wind energy developments
  • Population genetics studies using microsatellites or SNP markers
  • Long-term monitoring programmes using standardised methods
  • Method development — new primers, sample types and organisms
  • Ballast water analysis for the shipping industry
  • Parasite detection — for example, schistosomiasis through water eDNA
  • DNA barcoding and reference libraries
  • eDNA sampling training for your staff
