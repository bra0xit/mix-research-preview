MIX RESEARCH / SERVICE NOTE

Protected species detection

Verify the presence of red-listed species for environmental impact assessments and permitting.

01SERVICE
02THE QUESTION

Confirm the presence of red-listed species — quickly and reliably

Construction, infrastructure and wind energy projects often require evidence of protected species. Species-specific eDNA analysis can verify the presence of red-listed species with high sensitivity — without disturbing them.

  • Freshwater pearl mussel and other freshwater mussels (thick-shelled river mussel, painter’s mussel)
  • Amphibians — great crested newt, common spadefoot toad, European fire-bellied toad
  • Fish — Wels catfish, asp, stone loach, European river lamprey
  • Marine mammals — harbour porpoise, harbour seal, grey seal
  • Birds — through eDNA in water samples
  • Higher detection probability than conventional surveys
  • Non-invasive — no organisms need to be caught or handled
  • Faster and more cost-effective than electrofishing or diving surveys
  • Standardised methods that provide evidence for regulatory processes
  • Can be used throughout the year (sampling adapted to the species)
