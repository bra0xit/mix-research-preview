MIX RESEARCH / SERVICE NOTE

Invasive species surveys

Detect invasive species early and map their spread with high sensitivity.

01SERVICE
02THE QUESTION

Detect invasive species before they establish

Invasive species are among the greatest threats to biodiversity. Early detection is critical — the sooner an invasive species is found, the better the chance of stopping its spread. eDNA analysis can identify invasive species with very high sensitivity, often before they can be seen.

  • Signal crayfish — a threat to native noble crayfish
  • Round goby — spreading rapidly in the Baltic Sea
  • Zebra mussel and quagga mussel
  • Fringed water lily and other invasive aquatic plants
  • Spanish slug
  • Japanese knotweed and Himalayan balsam through soil eDNA
  • Can detect species at very low population densities
  • Faster and more affordable than conventional surveys
  • Broad geographical coverage with relatively few samples
  • Standardised and repeatable — enables comparisons over time
  • Can be integrated into existing environmental monitoring programmes
