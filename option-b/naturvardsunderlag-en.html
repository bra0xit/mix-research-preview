Services / Ecosystems

Evidence for conservation

Compare and prioritise conservation areas. Measure the effects of interventions over time.

Data-driven decisions for better conservation

Effective conservation requires reliable data. Our eDNA analyses provide the evidence you need to prioritise conservation, compare areas and measure the effects of interventions over time.

By combining biodiversity data with GIS analysis and ecological expertise, we deliver reports ready for use in decision-making — from municipal conservation planning and national monitoring programmes to individual conservation projects.

We work closely with regional authorities, municipalities, conservation organisations and consultancies to ensure our analyses meet your specific needs and requirements.

Need biodiversity data to make better conservation decisions?

We help you prioritise interventions, evaluate outcomes and document biodiversity value. Contact us for a free consultation.

Book a consultation →

What we offer

  • Comparative biodiversity analyses between areas — identify hotspots
  • Monitoring restoration outcomes — measure the effects of fish passages, wetland restoration and more
  • Baseline surveys — document current conditions before development or intervention
  • Ecological status assessment under the EU Water Framework Directive
  • Long-term monitoring using standardised methods
  • Maps with GIS-based visualisation of results

How we deliver

Study design — we help design your study to provide useful evidence for decisions. Sites, sample numbers, timelines and analytical methods are tailored to your questions.

Fieldwork — we collect samples or provide kits and instructions for your staff. We also offer training.

Report with recommendations — clear results with interpretation, comparisons and practical recommendations you can act on. All reports include maps and visualisations.

Typical clients

  • Regional authorities and municipalities — conservation planning and follow-up
  • Swedish Environmental Protection Agency — national monitoring programmes
  • Water management associations — ecological status assessment
  • Consultancies — evidence for environmental impact assessments and permitting
  • Research institutions — scientific studies
  • Energy companies — environmental evidence for wind and hydropower
