MIX RESEARCH / SERVICE NOTE

Individual identification from snow tracks

Identify individual animals, sex and relatedness — lynx, polar bears, wolverines and otters.

01SERVICE
02THE QUESTION

Identify individual animals — without seeing them

MIX Research pioneers eDNA analysis from snow tracks. By collecting snow from animal tracks, we can extract DNA and identify the species, determine the animal’s sex and even distinguish individuals.

  • Polar bear — individual identification and population estimates in the Arctic
  • Lynx — population monitoring through snow-track surveys
  • Wolverine — mapping individuals in mountain environments
  • Otter — individual identification from tracks along waterways
  • Snow leopard — pilot projects with international partners
  • Wolf and bear — a complementary method to scat surveys
