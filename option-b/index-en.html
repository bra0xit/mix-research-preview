03THE SCIENCE
ENVIRONMENTAL DNA / eDNA

The invisible
is essential.

A new approach to environmental analysis.

All organisms leave traces of DNA in their surroundings. By analysing these traces, we can identify which species are present — without catching or even seeing them.
